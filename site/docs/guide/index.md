# Guide

This guide walks through the web UI lanstash serves on its admin port, page by page, and the commands that go
with it. It assumes lanstash is installed, configured and reached through your DNS; if not, start with
[Getting started](../getting-started/index.md).

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Overview](overview.md) | The status line, the resource tiles, what clients got from the stash, the traffic chart, and the command palette. |
| [Platforms and games](platforms.md) | A platform's page and its parts, a game's page, and what Steam, Battle.net, Epic Games and Riot each need. |
| [Prefill](prefill.md) | Putting games into the stash ahead of time, the queue, automatic updates, and installs for another platform or language. |
| [Clients](clients.md) | Who downloads what, how much came from the stash, and naming the PCs on your network. |
| [History](history.md) | What was updated when: new builds, games added, and builds the stash came to hold whole. |
| [Accounts](accounts.md) | Roles and permissions, signing in, invites, linking a Steam account to a player, and HTTPS for the web UI. |
| [Hidden and private games](privacy.md) | Hiding a game from everyone, and a player keeping its own games to itself. |
| [Health and checks](health.md) | The Health page, `lanstash doctor`, checksums, `lanstash store verify`, the background check, and eviction. |

!!! tip
    Press ++ctrl+k++ or ++slash++ on any page to jump to a game or a page by typing its name.
